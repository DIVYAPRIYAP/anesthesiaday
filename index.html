Competition Categories

Category A

Children & Students

Topic: "My Future Profession"

For students up to 10th standard (Ages ~10-16). Paint your dreams and what you want to become!

Category B

Non-Medicos / Public

Topic: "Invisible Guardians"

Unsung Heroes in any field. Dedicated to the invisible guardians working behind the screens.

Category C

Medicos (Students/Doctors)

Topic: "Anaesthetist as a SUPERHERO!"

Design an epic battle! Your superhero vs Super Villains like Hypercarbia or Malignant Hyperthermia. Design your own gadgets!

Rules & Regulations

  • Paper Size: Artwork must be drawn on standard A3 or A4 size chart or drawing paper.
  • Medium: You can use Watercolors, Poster colors, Crayons, Color pencils, or Acrylics. Traditional art only.
  • No Digital Editing: Submit a clear photograph or scan. NO digital editing, filters, or color grading is allowed. Edited entries will be disqualified.
  • Originality: Must be your original imagination. Plagiarism is strictly prohibited.
  • Category C Note: Medicos must provide a brief description (in the form below) explaining their superhero gadgets and villains.
